Our Services

What We Offer

We provide expert services across audit, tax, corporate law, and litigation to help businesses thrive in Pakistan's evolving regulatory landscape.

Audit & Assurance

Comprehensive audit services for public and private limited companies, ensuring financial accuracy and regulatory compliance with applicable standards.

Tax Advisory & Compliance

Expert guidance on corporate taxation, income tax planning, sales tax, customs duties, and obtaining tax relief or exemptions from authorities.

Tax Litigation

Appeals against adverse tax orders, adjudication, and representation before tax authorities and senior courts including the Supreme Court of Pakistan.

Corporate Law Compliance

Company law compliance services including SECP filings, annual returns, board meeting documentation, and statutory record maintenance.

Foreign Investment Advisory

Assistance with company registration, licensing, legal compliance, and advisory on corporate and fiscal laws for foreign investors in Pakistan.

Import/Export Taxation

Specialized tax advisory for import and export businesses, including customs valuation, regulatory duties, and trade compliance matters.

NGO & NPO Services

Registration, compliance, and governance advisory for not-for-profit associations and non-governmental organizations operating in Pakistan.

Financial Reporting

Interim and annual financial reporting services ensuring compliance with applicable accounting frameworks and international standards.

Sustainability Counseling

ESG advisory and sustainability reporting to help businesses align with global environmental, social, and governance best practices.
